## Series 1 — Cannabis-Resin (Hashish) THC Potency in Europe, 2006–2023

**Source:** Freeman et al. (2019), *Addiction* (doi:10.1111/add.14525) for 2006–2016; EMCDDA/EUDA European Drug Reports 2023 & 2025 for 2021 and 2023.
**Method:** Mean THC% of seized cannabis-resin samples pooled across reporting EU member states.
**Caveat:** Values are pooled multi-country means; individual country ranges vary widely (e.g. 2021 range 12–29%). The 2006 figure (8.14%) and 2016 figure (17.22%) are from Freeman et al. peer-reviewed study. The 2011 inflection (~10%) and 2013 (~12%), 2018 (~19%), 2021 (~24%), and 2023 (23%) are from EMCDDA/EUDA reports or well-sourced summaries thereof. Intermediate years 2007–2010 estimated by interpolation within the Freeman dataset's documented "minimal change" period; do not use as standalone precise figures. The 2019–2020 range reported as 20–28% — central estimate used.

| Year | Mean THC % | Notes |
|------|-----------|-------|
| 2006 | 8.1 | Freeman et al. 2019 mean |
| 2008 | 8.5 | Interpolated (minimal change period per Freeman) |
| 2010 | 9.5 | Interpolated (minimal change period per Freeman) |
| 2011 | 10.0 | Freeman et al. approximate inflection point |
| 2013 | 12.5 | Approximate; marked increase began post-2011 per Freeman |
| 2016 | 17.2 | Freeman et al. 2019 mean |
| 2019 | 24.0 | EMCDDA; range reported as 20–28%, central estimate used |
| 2021 | 24.0 | EMCDDA EDR 2023; typical gram = 12–29% range |
| 2023 | 23.0 | EUDA European Drug Report 2025 |

## Series 2 — Global Cannabis Users Over Time, 2010–2024

**Source:** UNODC World Drug Reports (2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025 editions); figures published annually, each reporting on data from 2–3 years prior.
**Caveat:** These are estimates derived from survey harmonization across 130+ countries; methodology changes between report editions mean figures are not always directly comparable year-to-year. The 2010 and 2020 figures come from the 2022 WDR; the 2021 and 2022 figures from the 2023 and 2024 WDRs; the 2023/2024 figure from WDR 2025. Intermediate years 2012–2018 are not precisely stated in public summaries — do not interpolate as precise data. Percentage-of-adults figure for 2023 (4.6%) is from WDR 2025.

## Series 3 — Cannabis Resin Seizures by Top EU Country (2021 vs 2023)

**Source:** EMCDDA European Drug Reports 2023 (2021 data) and 2025 (2023 data); EUDA trafficking-and-supply pages.
**Caveat:** Figures reflect seizures reported by EU member states to EMCDDA; actual trafficking volumes are larger. Spain's share is so dominant because it is the main entry point from Morocco. Non-EU countries (Morocco, Turkey) are excluded from EU totals. 2021 country breakdown is more complete; for 2023 only Spain and France estimates are available in open sources.

## Series 4 — Estimated Cannabis Resin Production by Country

**Source:** UNODC World Drug Reports and crop-monitoring surveys; Morocco figures from UNODC 2003 survey (via press.un.org 2003) and UNODC WDR 2022; Afghanistan figures from UNODC Afghanistan Cannabis Survey 2009/2010.
**Caveat (IMPORTANT):** Production figures are highly uncertain estimates. Morocco's illicit production is not directly measured; it is inferred from cultivation area surveys and yield assumptions. Afghanistan figures (1,500–3,500 t/year as of ~2010 UNODC survey) have not been updated with equivalent granularity in recent years. Morocco's 73,000 ha figure (2021) is for illicit cultivation; the 2023 "294 tonnes" refers to newly legal medical cannabis only, not illicit resin production. Use ranges, not point estimates, when presenting to audiences.

| Country | Estimated Resin Production | Year / Period | Source | Caveat |
|---------|--------------------------|--------------|--------|--------|
| Morocco | ~3,080 t (peak) | 2003 | UNODC survey via UN Press | Historical peak; illicit only |
| Morocco | ~760 t | 2010 | UNODC crop monitoring | Illicit resin; sharp decline from 2003 |
| Morocco | ~1,000–2,000 t (est.) | 2020–2022 | UNODC WDR 2022 / EUDA | Range only; no precise survey |
| Afghanistan | 1,500–3,500 t/yr | ~2009 | UNODC Afghanistan Cannabis Survey 2010 | Wide range; 2nd largest global producer |
| Lebanon | < 500 t | 2010s | UNODC WDR (various) | Minor producer; no precise annual survey |
| Netherlands | Domestic herb; resin negligible | — | EMCDDA | NL produces herbal cannabis, not resin |

## Series 5 — Cannabis Policy Reform: Key Global Milestones Timeline

**Source:** Wikipedia "Timeline of cannabis law"; UN Single Convention (1961); news sources for 2012–2024.
**Caveat:** "Decriminalization," "medical legalization," and "full recreational legalization" are legally distinct but often conflated. US state-level reforms are not counted as national reforms here. The cumulative "countries with medical or full access" count is approximate as definitions vary.

| Year | Milestone | Type | Jurisdiction |
|------|-----------|------|-------------|
| 1961 | UN Single Convention on Narcotic Drugs — cannabis scheduled globally | Prohibition codified | International |
| 1972 | Shafer Commission (US) recommends decriminalization; Nixon rejects it | Reform proposal rejected | USA |
| 1976 | Netherlands tolerance policy (gedoogbeleid) — de facto decrim in coffeeshops | Decriminalization (de facto) | Netherlands |
| 2001 | Portugal decriminalizes all drugs including cannabis | Decriminalization | Portugal |
| 2012 | Colorado & Washington legalize recreational cannabis (first anywhere) | Full legalization | US (state) |
| 2013 | Uruguay becomes first nation to fully legalize recreational cannabis | Full national legalization | Uruguay |
| 2018 | Canada legalizes recreational cannabis nationally | Full national legalization | Canada |
| 2021 | Malta legalizes adult-use cannabis (first in EU); Mexico decriminalizes | Full legalization / Decrim | Malta / Mexico |
| 2022 | Thailand removes cannabis from narcotics list | Decriminalization | Thailand |
| 2023 | Germany passes legalization law (takes effect 2024); Luxembourg legalizes | Full legalization | Germany / Luxembourg |
| 2024 | Germany recreational legalization fully in effect | Full national legalization | Germany |

## Summary of Sources and Caveats (< 250 words)

**Series 1 (THC potency, Europe):** Core data from Freeman et al. (2019), a peer-reviewed EMCDDA-linked study in *Addiction*, covering 2006–2016 with country-pooled means. Post-2016 data from EMCDDA European Drug Reports (2023, 2025). The 2006 (8.1%) and 2016 (17.2%) values are the most precisely sourced; intermediate years partly interpolated. 2023 figure (23%) is directly stated in EUDA's 2025 report.

**Series 2 (Global cannabis users):** All figures from UNODC World Drug Reports (2022–2025). The time series is sparse — only five confirmed anchor points (2010, 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023/24). Percentage-of-adults figure (4.6%) is specifically from WDR 2025.

**Series 3 (EU seizures by country):** Drawn from EMCDDA European Drug Reports. The 2021 breakdown (Spain 672.5 t, France 72.5 t, Italy 20 t, EU total 816 t) and 2023 figures (Spain 375 t, EU total 551 t) are directly cited. The 2023 per-country breakdown for France and Italy is estimated from proportional shares.

**Series 4 (Production by country):** Most uncertain series. Morocco's 2003 peak (3,080 t) and 2010 figure (760 t) are from UNODC surveys; 2020–22 range is inferred, not measured. Afghanistan's 1,500–3,500 t range is from a single 2009 survey. Present as ranges with explicit uncertainty.

**Series 5 (Policy milestones):** Compiled from the UN Single Convention text (1961), Wikipedia "Timeline of cannabis law," and news coverage of national legislation 2012–2024.
